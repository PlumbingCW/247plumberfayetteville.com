About Fayetteville 24/7 Plumbing

Call (770) 214-4545
  • Licensed and insured
  • Clear price before work starts
  • Same-day appointments when available

Fayetteville 24/7 Plumbing is a local plumbing service built around one idea: when water is where it should not be, someone should answer the phone. The dispatch line is open every hour of every day, and licensed plumbers take the jobs.

This page explains how we work, what you can expect on a visit and how to check us out before you let anyone in.

What we do

We repair and install plumbing for homes and small businesses in Fayetteville, GA and nearby Peachtree City. The work runs from drain cleaning and leak detection to water heater replacement and sewer line repair.

Our office is at 365 Fayette Place, Fayetteville, GA 30214. Everything is arranged by phone, so there are no web forms to fill out.

How pricing works

The plumber looks at the problem and explains the options. You get a clear price before work starts, and the work begins only after you agree.

Price depends on the fixture, access to the pipe, parts, and whether the visit is after hours. We do not quote numbers over the phone for problems we have not seen, because a wrong number helps no one.

Licensed and insured, and how to verify it

Our plumbers are licensed and insured. You do not have to take our word for it, and you should not take anyone's. According to the Georgia Secretary of State, plumbers are licensed through the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, and its online Licensee Search lets you verify a name in minutes.

What a visit looks like

A plumber arrives, listens to what you saw and heard, and then checks the fixture, the pipe or the appliance. Before anything is taken apart, the plumber tells you what is wrong in plain words and what the options are.

You see the price before the work starts. If the plumber finds a second problem, you hear about it before it becomes part of the job, and you decide whether to add it now or later.

When the work is finished, the plumber runs the water, checks for leaks and shows you what changed. You also learn where your main shutoff valve is, because that single valve is the best tool any homeowner has.

Homes and businesses we work on

Most calls come from houses, townhomes and condos, and the work runs from a stuck toilet to a full repipe. We also take calls from shops, restaurants, offices and rental properties. The commercial plumbing page explains what is different about after-hours work for a business.

Fayetteville has grown fast since 1990, so many homes here have water heaters, supply lines and fixtures that are at the age when parts start to fail. A plumber who knows the age of the house can often guess where to look first.

Where we work

We serve Fayetteville ZIP codes 30214 and 30215 and cover Peachtree City, including 30269. The Peachtree City page explains how its sewer service differs from Fayetteville's, and the service areas page lists everything we cover.

If your town is not listed, call anyway. We would rather tell you honestly that a plumber cannot reach you than leave you waiting.

How to get ready before you call

Find the main shutoff valve before you need it. It is usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house. Learn which way it turns, and make sure it moves, because a valve that has not been touched in years can stick.

Keep a flashlight, a bucket and a few towels near the water heater and under the kitchen sink. Write the phone number on the fridge. When something does go wrong at night, those small steps buy you time.

Why we use the phone and not a form

A form promises a reply and then leaves you waiting with water on the floor. A phone call puts you in front of a dispatcher in seconds, and the dispatcher can ask the questions that matter: where is the water coming from, can you reach the shutoff, is anyone in danger.

That is also why this website has no chat widget or contact form. Everything points to one number, (770) 214-4545, and the line is answered at every hour. For anything that is not urgent, email works, and replies come the next business day.

If you are comparing options before you call, read the pages on the plumbing services page to see how each repair works and what drives its cost.

What we will not do

  • We will not promise a response time we cannot keep.
  • We will not push a replacement when a repair is enough.
  • We will not advertise reviews, ratings or awards we do not hold.
  • We will not work on gas leaks as an emergency service. Leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility first.

Our service area

Dispatch runs from Fayetteville in Fayette County, with crews also covering Peachtree City and nearby towns along Highway 54 and Highway 85.
