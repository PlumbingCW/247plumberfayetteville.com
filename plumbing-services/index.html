How to choose the right service

Start with the symptom. Water spreading from a pipe or a tank is a repair call. A slow drain is a cleaning call. An old heater with no leak is usually a planned replacement.

If the problem looks like it could be two things, such as a wet ceiling that is either a roof leak or a supply line, ask for leak detection first. Finding the source before repairing saves wasted work.
