ZIP codes we cover

According to the USPS, Fayetteville has ZIP codes 30214 and 30215, and we cover both, including the Highway 85, Highway 54 and Highway 92 corridors and the Banks Crossing and Fayette Pavilion shopping areas. In Peachtree City, we cover 30269, 30270 and 31169.

Those ZIP codes come from the USPS. Peachtree City's 30270 and 31169 are assigned to single organizations, so most home addresses there use 30269.

Towns we mention but do not list as covered

Tyrone, Brooks, Woolsey, Lovejoy and Sharpsburg all lie near our main area. Brooks, for example, has its own public water system, separate from the county system. If you live in one, call and ask whether we can reach you before you assume either way.

How coverage works after midnight

The dispatch line is staffed around the clock, so coverage does not change at night. Whether the call comes at noon or at 3 a.m., a dispatcher confirms your address and books a licensed plumber.
