What home age changes, and what it does not

Two homes on the same street can be fifteen years apart. That shifts the maintenance list but does not mean any home has a defect. These are general checks, not findings about this community.

  • Older sections: check the water heater label. The U.S. Department of Energy says storage tank heaters commonly last 10 to 15 years, so water heater replacement may come up in the older homes.
  • All sections: replace worn toilet parts early. A leaking flapper can run unnoticed and waste water, so toilet repair is worth doing early.
  • Kitchens: a jammed disposal or a slow sink is a common call. See garbage disposal repair and drain cleaning.

HOA rules and shared lines

Read your HOA covenants before exterior work. Some rules cover visible equipment and contractor access. Also ask your HOA which lines it maintains and which ones belong to you. That answer decides who you call for a problem outside the house.

Choosing who works on your home

Georgia lets you check any plumber on the Georgia Secretary of State Licensee Search. Look the person up before work starts. For permits on a heater swap or pipe reroute, ask the Fayetteville building department.
