When a tank splits open at the base or the water has run cold for good, the question stops being how to fix it and becomes how fast you can get a new one in. The Fayetteville 24/7 Plumbing line is open around the clock, and while you wait you can close the cold valve above the tank and cut its power or gas control.

Water heater replacement in Fayetteville, GA is the removal of a failed or worn-out heater and the fitting of a new one, with the connections, venting, and safety devices brought up to a sound standard. This page covers how to judge whether a tank is finished, what the new unit could be, and what the visit involves.

No single rule fits every house. The plumber looks at age, leak location, and repair history together. If a fix is still sensible, you will hear that, and our repair service covers it.

Water spreading around a dead tank

A failed tank can release dozens of gallons before anyone notices, so the first ten minutes count. Close the cold valve on the supply pipe, switch off the breaker or turn the gas control to off, and open a hot tap somewhere to relieve pressure. Never touch a wet electrical connection.

While you wait for the plumber

Lift valuables and cardboard off the floor and use towels to guide water toward a floor drain. Photograph the unit, its label, and any damage before cleanup begins.

Those photos help later with an insurance claim and let the technician see the model before arriving. A night or weekend call goes to the same 24/7 dispatch line, and same-day appointments are offered when available.

What age and sediment do to a tank

Inside a steel tank, an anode rod sacrifices itself to slow rust. Once it is used up, corrosion goes after the tank wall. Meanwhile minerals settle on the bottom, insulate the water from the burner or lower element, and make the tank work harder.

How fast that happens depends on the water. Fayetteville's water comes about half from three city wells and the rest from the Fayette County Water System, and neither published report gives a hardness figure. Test your tap, or ask the water utility for its annual water quality report, before choosing a model that depends on clean water.

Many Fayetteville homes went up in the 1990s and 2000s, which means plenty of original heaters have already outlived a typical tank. If yours has a date on the label from that era, expect replacement to come up soon, planned or not.

A like-for-like swap is the quickest path after a failure. Changing fuel type, moving the heater, or going tankless involves extra work, and the installation page explains those projects. For the tankless route in particular, see the tankless unit page.

Sizing the new heater to how you use hot water

A heater that is too small runs out mid-shower. One that is too large wastes energy keeping extra water hot. Size depends on the number of people, how many bathrooms run at once, and whether you have a soaking tub or a large shower.

Tell the plumber what happened with your old unit. If you kept running out, a bigger tank or a different type may fit better. If hot water was always plenty, matching the old capacity is usually fine.

The replacement visit from shutoff to hot taps

  • Drain and disconnect: the old tank is emptied and detached from water, gas or power, and venting.
  • New connections: fresh supply lines, a shutoff valve, and a relief valve with a discharge pipe routed safely.
  • Expansion control: an expansion tank is added where the plumbing calls for one.
  • Venting and gas check: gas units get their vent and connections checked and tested for leaks.
  • Startup and removal: the unit is filled, purged of air, brought to temperature, and the old tank is hauled off.

Permit rules vary, so ask the Fayetteville building department before a water heater swap. The plumber can tell you what the job involves. Homes across 30214 and 30215, the Fayetteville ZIP codes this work covers, follow the same city process.

What shapes the quote, and what insurance does

Cost depends on tank size, fuel type, the model you pick, venting changes, code updates to old connections, and how hard the heater is to reach. A good quote itemizes the unit, labor, parts, and haul-away. Ask what is included before work starts.

Homeowners insurance generally responds to sudden accidental damage, like a burst tank flooding a room, but not to wear and tear or the failed heater itself. Policies differ, so read yours. To get a price for your house, call (770) 214-4545.
