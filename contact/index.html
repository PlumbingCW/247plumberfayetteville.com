Contact Fayetteville 24/7 Plumbing

The fastest way to reach Fayetteville 24/7 Plumbing is the phone. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year, and a dispatcher books a licensed plumber while you talk.

We do not use contact forms. A call gets a real person faster than a message waiting in an inbox.

What to have ready when you call

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether you have found and shut off the main water valve.
  • Any photos you have taken for insurance.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility before you call us.

Office address and email
