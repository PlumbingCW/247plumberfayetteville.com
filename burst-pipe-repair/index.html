A burst pipe sends water into a house fast, and every minute adds to the damage. Burst pipe repair in Fayetteville, GA starts the moment you stop the flow, and our 24/7 dispatch line is open for exactly that. Shut the main valve now, then read on while you wait for the plumber.

Pipes split for several reasons: freezing, age, a failed fitting or a supply valve that lets go. The fix depends on the pipe material and where the break sits. Below is what to do in the first minutes, how each type of pipe is repaired and what the cleanup involves.

The first minutes after a pipe lets go

  1. 1

    Close the main shutoff

    It is usually where the line enters the house, near the water heater or at an exterior wall. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

  2. 2

    Open the lowest faucet

    This drains what is left in the pipes so less water reaches the break.

  3. 3

    Cut power if water is near electricity

    Switch off the breaker for that area only if you can reach the panel on dry ground. Otherwise stay out and tell dispatch.

  4. 4

    Move what you can

    Lift rugs, electronics and paper off the floor, and photograph the damage before cleanup.

  5. 5

    Call the 24/7 line

    A same-day appointment is available when the schedule allows. Dispatch can talk you through anything you cannot find.

Do not wait to see if the water slows. A split pipe under pressure does not repair itself, and the main is the one valve that always works.

Cracked copper, PEX and drain pipe repaired three different ways

How the pipe material changes the repair
Pipe typeHow it usually failsTypical repair
Copper supplyPinholes, split seams or a joint that lets goCut out the bad section and sweat in new pipe, or use fittings
PEX supplyCracks at fittings or rodent and impact damageCut and replace with new fittings or a new run
Galvanized steelRust from the inside, then a sudden breakReplace the section, often a sign to plan repiping
Drain or waste pipeCracked joint or crushed sectionReplace the pipe segment and test for slow drips afterward

Drain pipes leak differently than supply pipes. Water leaks only when something drains, so a broken drain can stay hidden for weeks. A cracked drain pipe repair usually means cutting out the damaged piece and fitting a new length with proper slope.

Cold nights and split lines

Hard freezes happen in some winters here, and on the coldest nights water inside pipes can freeze and expand until the pipe splits. The damage often appears only when the ice thaws and water starts flowing. Pipes in an unheated crawlspace, an exterior wall or a garage are the usual victims.

If a frozen pipe has not burst yet, keep the faucet open a little and warm the area gently. Never use an open flame. If water is already spraying, the main goes off first. A line that has frozen once is worth having checked, since cracks can hide behind a closed wall.

Burst supply valves, stop valves and flexible lines

Not every burst involves a pipe in the wall. Braided supply hoses under a sink, toilet connectors and the little stop valve beneath a fixture can fail without warning. These are small parts with big consequences, because a cracked hose can flood a kitchen in an hour.

The quick fix is the fixture shutoff, a small oval handle on the supply line near the wall. If it will not turn or it leaks around the handle, close the main. Replacing a failed valve is usually fast, and a good plumber replaces the hose or connector at the same time since it has the same age.

After the repair: drying, photos and insurance

The plumber fixes the pipe, but wet materials still need attention. Drywall, insulation and flooring can hold water long after the leak stops. Ask whether the area needs to be opened for drying, and consider a restoration company for large spills.

  • Take photos and video of the wet area, the broken pipe and any damaged belongings before anything is moved.
  • Keep the failed part if the plumber removes it, since an insurer may ask to see it.
  • Write down the time you noticed the problem and when you closed the main.
  • Save every receipt for the repair and any drying work.

Homeowners policies generally respond to sudden accidental water damage. They usually do not pay for wear and tear or for the cost of replacing the failed pipe itself, and policies differ, so check yours.

Opening a wall and what gets patched

Reaching a break behind drywall means cutting an opening, and the plumber will try to keep it small. A listening tool or thermal camera can place the cut close to the leak. When the break is not obvious, leak detection comes first so the opening lands in the right place.

The plumber closes the pipe, not the wall. Drywall patching, texture and paint usually belong to a handyman or contractor, so ask before work begins who will handle the finish. Burst pipes in homes in 30214 and 30215 follow the same process regardless of ZIP, with timing based on the schedule.
